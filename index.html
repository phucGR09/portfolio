About Alex Morgan

I’m a designer who enjoys turning complicated systems into calm, approachable experiences. I currently work independently with early-stage teams and established organizations on product strategy, interaction design, and design systems.

My best work happens when I can listen closely, ask practical questions, and build with the people who will use the result. Away from a screen, I’m usually looking for a good book, an even better coffee, or a long walk with no destination.
